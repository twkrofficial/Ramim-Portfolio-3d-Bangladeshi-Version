# Bangladeshi Rickshaw & Rickshaw Art — Visual Design Research & Low-Poly Modeling Spec

## 1. Anatomy of the Dhaka Cycle Rickshaw (tricycle passenger rickshaw)

### Layout
Driver in front pedaling a bicycle-style front half; passengers behind on a raised bench over the two rear wheels. One front wheel, two rear wheels, chain drive to the rear axle. Design essentially unchanged since the 1930s ([Wikipedia — Cycle rickshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cycle_rickshaw), [Context BD — Rickshaw Revisited](https://contextbd.com/rickshaw-revisited/)).

### Parts checklist (each maps to a primitive)

| Part | Description | Primitive suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Front wheel | Bicycle wheel, ~26" (≈66 cm) dia, thin 1.5" tire, wire spokes | Torus + flat disc or 8-spoke cylinder fan |
| Rear wheels (x2) | Same ~26" size or slightly smaller (18–20" on some builds), partly hidden by body/mudguards | Same as front |
| Frame | Heavy black-painted steel tube: bicycle front half + rear cradle. Curved "swan-neck" tubes from seat down to rear axle | Bent cylinders, 6–8 sided |
| Front fork + handlebar | Standard bicycle fork; wide swept-back handlebar with bell, often chromed, plastic streamer tassels at grips | Cylinders |
| Saddle | Sprung leather bicycle saddle for the puller | Flattened box/wedge |
| Passenger bench | Padded bench ~90 cm wide, upholstered in shiny rexine/vinyl, classically red or maroon with silver piping; tufted or striped | Box with rounded top edge |
| Backrest | Slightly reclined padded panel behind bench, vinyl with appliqué flowers | Tilted box |
| Folding hood ("kamani") | THE signature silhouette: made of ~5 bamboo/steel strips hinged at the sides so it folds like a pram hood. Raised, it forms a half-clamshell arc curving up and over the bench, with a scalloped valance flap ("cap") extending forward like a sun visor. Covered in black or colored rexine/vinyl, densely decorated with appliqué | 4–5 nested curved bands (lathe/arc extrusions), each slightly larger, + a scalloped front flap plane ([Context BD](https://contextbd.com/rickshaw-revisited/)) |
| Footboard | Low step/board for passengers ahead of the bench, painted with simple motifs | Flat box |
| Rear art board | Rectangular tin plate ~45 × 25 cm mounted low between the rear wheels — the classic painted "canvas" | Single flat quad with the hero texture ([Jupiter Pradhan — Rickshaw Art of Bangladesh](https://jupiterpradhan.wordpress.com/research/rickshaw-art-of-bangladesh/)) |
| Rear body panel / mudguards | Curved sheet-metal skirts over rear wheels + rear apron, all painted with borders and flowers | Curved shells (quarter cylinders) |
| Side panels | Painted boards flanking the seat/footwell | Flat quads |
| Decorations | Tinsel garlands, plastic flowers, mirror discs, tassels along hood edge and handlebar | Tiny spheres/cards, emissive-ish accents |

### Rough proportions (stylized target)
- Overall: length ~2.4–2.6 m, width ~1.1–1.2 m, height with hood up ~1.8–2.0 m. (BUET's "same-size" modern replacement is 3.2 × 1.5 × 2.1 m, so the traditional one is a bit smaller — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/city/70f4qp2uka).)
- Wheels: ~0.66–0.70 m diameter, all three roughly equal ([TradeIndia listings](https://www.tradeindia.com/manufacturers/cycle-rickshaw.html), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cycle_rickshaw)).
- Bench seat height ~0.75 m off ground; hood apex ~1.9 m; rear track ~0.9 m; wheelbase (front axle to rear axle) ~1.6 m.
- Good stylized ratios: wheel dia = 1 unit → length 3.6 u, hood apex 2.8 u, bench width 1.6 u.

## 2. Rickshaw Art (UNESCO ICH, inscribed Dec 2023)

"Rickshaws and rickshaw painting in Dhaka" inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List at the 18th session, Kasane, Dec 2023 ([UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/rickshaws-and-rickshaw-painting-dhaka-inscribed-unesco-representative-list-intangible-cultural), [Decision 18.COM 8.B.23](https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/18.COM/8.B.23), [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/life-living/news/streets-heritage-unesco-recognises-rickshaws-and-rickshaw-art-intangible-cultural-heritage-3490731)).

### Style & palette
Enamel paint on recycled tin; hyper-saturated, flat, hard-outlined pop-folk style. Magenta and green enamels dominant since the early 1980s; fluorescents popular from the mid-1980s ([Jupiter Pradhan](https://jupiterpradhan.wordpress.com/research/rickshaw-art-of-bangladesh/)). Working hexes: hot magenta/pink `#E4007C`, vermilion red `#E23B2E`, cobalt/ultramarine `#1F3FA8`, leaf green `#1FA637`, chrome yellow `#FFC907`, sky cyan `#33B6E5`, white outlines, black keylines.

### Motifs
- Flowers — roses, lotus, and the national flower shapla (water lily); garland borders ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/experiences/heritage/the-rickshaw-art-of-bangladesh-is-now-unesco-intangible-heritage))
- Birds — peacocks and doves/pigeons; animals (tigers, fanciful hybrids)
- Cinema stars — Dhallywood faces with sunglasses and big hair (mid-70s onward)
- Architecture — Taj Mahal-like domes, mosques, famous buildings
- Rural rivers-and-village scenes, Liberation War heroes, text panels ([Bangladesh Branding](https://bangladeshbranding.com/rickshaw-art-a-dynamic-expression-of-bangladeshs-rich-heritage/), [Wikipedia — Rickshaw painting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickshaw_art))

### Where each decoration sits
- **Rear tin board (45 × 25 cm)** — the hero painting: movie star, peacock, Taj dome, or village scene, framed by a painted border ([Jupiter Pradhan](https://jupiterpradhan.wordpress.com/research/rickshaw-art-of-bangladesh/))
- **Hood (outer surface + back)** — appliqué, not paint: colored/gold/silver plastic cut-outs in "Dhaka design" medallions centered on a red or blue rose, a peacock, or a candle, ringed by golden butterflies, stars and crescents; scalloped gold trim on the valance
- **Seat + backrest** — vinyl appliqué flowers, piping
- **Side panels, mudguards, footboard, frame tubes** — floral scrolls, lotus buds, striping; even hubs and spokes get color
- **Everywhere** — tassels, tinsel, plastic flowers on handlebar and hood edges ([UNESCO article](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/rickshaw-painting-colourful-heritage-bangladesh))

### Typical hood colorways
Black vinyl base with red/gold appliqué (classic Dhaka); or fully colored hoods in red, royal blue, or green vinyl with contrasting scalloped silver/gold borders. Hoods are trending narrower for weight/wind reasons ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/why-are-rickshaw-hoods-dhaka-getting-narrower-835111)).

## 3. Green CNG Auto-Rickshaw ("baby taxi" / "CNG") — secondary skin

- Shape: Bajaj-RE-type three-wheeler — one small front wheel under a rounded snub nose, single headlamp, canopy cabin with steel-mesh/grille side openings, hard roof, rear bench behind driver, roughly 2.6 m long × 1.3 m wide × 1.7 m high. Model as: rounded nose box + cabin box with cut windows + three small wheels (~0.3 m dia).
- Color: all commercial units painted a uniform medium green to brand them as eco-friendly CNG vehicles; "CNG" became the vehicle's everyday name. Predecessor baby taxis were black with yellow canvas tops. Only green ones may operate commercially in Dhaka; grey/silver ones are private ([CS Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/Environment/Living-Green/2009/0325/compressed-natural-gas-clears-the-air-in-bangladesh), [Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Compressed_Natural_Gas), [New Age](https://www.newagebd.net/article/127672/cng-run-pvt-auto-rickshaws-cant-operate-commercially-in-capital-hc)).
- Signature green hex (from photo reference, mid kelly-green with slight yellow cast): **`#2E8B2E`**; shaded panels `#1F6B24`; grey mesh grille `#6E7370`; yellow "CNG" rooftop sign / trim `#F5C518`.

### Modeling notes for the developer
- Build order: 3 wheels (cylinder+torus, 8 spokes each) → frame from 6-sided bent tubes → bench box + tilted backrest → hood as 4 concentric arc bands (largest at back, each ~8% smaller going forward) + one scalloped flap quad at the front edge → 45×25 cm rear quad for the art texture → quarter-cylinder mudguards → footboard.
- Paint strategy: flat vertex/material colors from the table; ONE small hand-painted texture only on the rear art board (peacock or shapla on cobalt with a yellow border) — that alone reads as "Dhaka rickshaw."
- Motion garnish: 3–5 tinsel tassel cards on handlebar ends and hood rim, jittered by a sine wave, sell the authenticity cheaply.
